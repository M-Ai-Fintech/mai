## Overview
The **Badge** component is a pill-shaped tag used to denote categories, M&A transaction states, and compliance alerts.

## Usage Guidelines

### Do's
- Use badges to highlight status (e.g., Sourcing, Due Diligence).
- Keep text short and uppercase.

### Don'ts
- Do not use badges as clickable interactive elements (use Buttons instead).
- Do not overload a single view with too many badges.

---

## Variants
- **Default**: Standard state.
- **Orange**: Signal Orange badge for alerts and KYB warnings.
- **Dark**: Dark theme badge.
- **Outline**: Outlined transparent badge.
