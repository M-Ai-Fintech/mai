## Overview
The **Button** component is the primary atomic element for interactions and CTAs in the M&Ai system. It supports the Ink Black pill aesthetic (`20px radius`) for primary actions, the Signal Orange consent variation (`#CF4500` with mandatory pure white text/icon), and the circular Satellite Micro-CTA.

## Usage Guidelines

### Do's
- **Primary Ink Pill**: Use for main on-page CTAs ("Explore Deal", "View Analysis").
- **Consent Signal Orange**: Use for compliance actions, terms acceptance, or legal confirmation.
- **Satellite CTA**: Dock at the edge of circular service cards.

### Don'ts
- Do not use Signal Orange on standard marketing primary buttons.
- Do not remove the 20px border radius from pill buttons.

---

## Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

- **Touch Target**: Guaranteed minimum of **44x44px** (`md` variant) and **48x48px** (`lg`/`satellite`).
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Visible focus ring activated on `focus-visible`.
