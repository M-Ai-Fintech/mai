## Authority boundary

Figma file `otMJE31OKIxdXogV4RNbGQ` is the sole visual and interaction design authority. ## Inspection and decision method

Every tracked `gui/` path was enumerated with `git ls-files gui` and inspected individually. Text/module content was read and local imports were resolved to exact tracked importers. Binary snapshots were inspected as PNG files and checked for tracked runtime references. No directory name alone determined a disposition.

Pre-mutation inventory: **335 exact paths** — **44 KEEP**, **8 REPLACE**, **283 REMOVE**.

A REMOVE decision authorizes deletion only after its listed importers are removed or replaced, retained behavior is covered by KEEP/replacement paths, and the stated post-delete search plus focused/full checks pass.
