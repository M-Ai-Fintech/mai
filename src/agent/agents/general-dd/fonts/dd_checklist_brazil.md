# Comprehensive M&A Due Diligence Checklist (Brazil)

> **Target Audience:** Buy-Side Legal, Tax, and Financial Advisors, RAG Query
> Systems\
> **Core Focus:** Mandatory Certificates (Certidões) & Information Request Lists
> (IRL) for Brazilian Target Companies

Due diligence in Brazil requires strict verification of successor liability.
Under **Art. 133 of the National Tax Code (CTN)** and **Art. 448 of the
Consolidation of Labor Laws (CLT)**, the acquirer is legally bound by the
target's historic tax and labor liabilities, regardless of contractual
indemnities.

---

## 1. Mandatory Government Certificates Checklist

Below is the definitive list of official certificates (_Certidões_) that must be
pulled directly from government portals to verify the Target’s standing.

| Certificate Name                                                                       | Acronym                  | Issuing Authority                         | Validity    | RAG/DD Criticality                                                                                                    |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :---------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Certidão Conjunta de Débitos Relativos a Tributos Federais e Dívida Ativa da União** | CND Federal              | Receita Federal & PGFN                    | 180 days    | **Critical.** Verifies federal taxes (IRPJ, CSLL, PIS, COFINS) and Social Security contributions (INSS).              |
| **Certidão Negativa de Débitos Trabalhistas**                                          | CNDT                     | TST & CSJT                                | 180 days    | **Critical.** Confirms the absence of unpaid, final court-ordered labor settlements.                                  |
| **Certificado de Regularidade do FGTS**                                                | CRF FGTS                 | Caixa Econômica Federal                   | 30 days     | **High.** Essential for checking regular employee severance fund deposits.                                            |
| **Certidão de Falência e Recuperação Judicial**                                        | CND Falência             | local Tribunal de Justiça (Civil)         | 30-90 days  | **Critical.** Verifies if the target is in bankruptcy, judicial, or extrajudicial reorganization.                     |
| **Certidão Negativa de Tributos Estaduais**                                            | CND Estadual             | local Secretaria da Fazenda (SEFAZ)       | 90-180 days | **High.** Verifies state taxes (primarily ICMS). Required for each state where target operates.                       |
| **Certidão Negativa de Tributos Municipais**                                           | CND Municipal            | local Secretaria de Finanças / Prefeitura | 90 days     | **Medium.** Covers local taxes (ISS, IPTU).                                                                           |
| **Certidão de Distribuidores Cíveis, Criminais e Execuções Fiscais**                   | Certidão de Distribuição | Tribunal de Justiça (TJ) & TRF            | 30-90 days  | **High.** Maps ongoing lawsuits, criminal charges against directors, and tax foreclosure actions (_Execução Fiscal_). |
| **Certidão de Regularidade do Cadastro de Contribuintes (CADIN)**                      | CADIN                    | Banco Central do Brasil / PGFN            | Real-time   | **Medium.** Identifies pending payments to the federal public sector.                                                 |

### Types of Outcomes:

- **CND (Certidão Negativa de Débitos):** Complete clearance. No outstanding
  debts.
- **CPND (Certidão Positiva com Efeitos de Negativa):** Debts exist but are
  legally suspended. This occurs if:
  1. The debt is under an installment plan (_parcelamento_) and payments are
     current.
  2. The debt is guaranteed by a court deposit (_depósito judicial_) or a bank
     guarantee (_fiança bancária_).
  - _Action:_ Subagents must review the underlying case to evaluate the risk of
    suspension failure.
- **CPD (Certidão Positiva de Débitos):** Active debt without suspension.
  **Alerta Vermelho (Red Flag).** The target is in default.

## 2. Information Request List (IRL) - Functional Checklists

### 2.1. Corporate & Societário

- [ ] Contrato Social (consolidated) and all amendments registered at the Junta
      Comercial.
- [ ] Quotaholder Agreements and voting trust arrangements.
- [ ] List of all subsidiaries, affiliates, and joint ventures.
- [ ] Active Powers of Attorney (Procurações) indicating scope and expiration
      dates.
- [ ] Document proving registration of the Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) via
      **e-BEF** (Instrução Normativa RFB nº 2.290/2025).

### 2.2. Financial & Accounting

- [ ] Balance sheets (Balanço Patrimonial) and DRE for the past 5 years.
- [ ] General Ledger (Razão Social) and Trial Balances (Balancetes) for the
      trailing 12 months.
- [ ] Detail of accounts receivable, including an aging list and provisions for
      doubtful accounts (PECLD).
- [ ] Detailed debt schedule listing creditor, principal, interest rate,
      guarantees, covenants, and maturity.
- [ ] Reconciliation of bank accounts and short-term financial investments.

### 2.3. Tax & Fiscal

- [ ] SPED ECF, ECD, EFD Contributions, and EFD ICMS/IPI files for the last 5
      fiscal years.
- [ ] Detailed calculations of IRPJ, CSLL, PIS, COFINS, ISS, and ICMS.
- [ ] Evidence of tax benefits or special regimes (e.g., _Drawback_, _PROPOSTA_,
      or regional ICMS reductions).
- [ ] Detailed analysis of any ongoing administrative tax disputes (e.g., CARF
      or municipal councils).
- [ ] Documentation regarding intercompany transactions and transfer pricing
      compliance.

### 2.4. Labor & Previdenciário

- [ ] Employee register report from eSocial.
- [ ] Employee census detailing admission, function, current wage, last salary
      increase, and vacation schedule.
- [ ] Signed agreements with independent contractors (PJs) and specialized
      service providers.
- [ ] Collective Bargaining Agreements (Convenção Coletiva) applicable to the
      company.
- [ ] Documentation of workplace safety plans (PPRA, PCMSO) and payment of risk
      premiums (RAT/FAP).

### 2.5. Operational & Commercial

- [ ] Standard sales contracts and terms of service.
- [ ] Top 10 clients: Contracts, historical billing, and payment terms.
- [ ] Top 10 suppliers: Contracts, key terms, and dependencies.
- [ ] Map of strategic contracts containing change of control, assignability, or
      exclusivity clauses.

### 2.6. Intellectual Property & Tech

- [ ] List of trademarks, patents, and software registrations at the INPI.
- [ ] Software development agreements demonstrating assignment of intellectual
      property rights from developers (CLT and PJ) to the company.
- [ ] LGPD Compliance documentation: Data Protection Officer (DPO) designation,
      Privacy Policy, and Security Breach response protocols.

### 2.7. Environmental & Regulatory

- [ ] Environmental licenses (LP, LI, LO) and corresponding renewal applications
      (filed at least 120 days prior to expiration to ensure automatic extension
      under Complementary Law 140/2011).
- [ ] Municipal Operating License (Alvará de Funcionamento) and Fire Department
      Inspection Certificate (AVCB).
- [ ] Sanitary permits (Vigilância Sanitária) and sector-specific authorizations
      (ANVISA, MAPA, ANATEL, etc.).

## 3. Verification Protocols (Anti-Fraud)

1. **Direct Portal Validation:** Never rely solely on PDFs provided in the VDR.
   The due diligence team must independently pull and validate the authenticity
   of certificates using the QR code or verification key directly on the issuing
   agency's website (e.g., _Portal e-CAC_ for federal certificates).
2. **Active Litigation Sweeps:** Search for active lawsuits using the national
   CNJ database (**DataJud**), regional courts (TRTs for labor, TJs for state
   civil, TRFs for federal civil/tax), and search aggregators (Jusbrasil) using
   both the corporate CNPJ and the personal CPFs of key partners.
