# Materiality Thresholds (Linhas de Materialidade) in Brazilian M&A Due Diligence

This document provides a framework for establishing materiality thresholds
(_linhas de materialidade_) during the legal due diligence (DD) of target
companies in Brazil. Establishing clear limits prevents _deal fatigue_ and
allows the transaction team to focus on liabilities that could materially impact
the target's valuation, purchase price, or post-closing risk allocation.

---

## 1. Materiality Architecture in Brazilian M&A

Due diligence findings are filtered through a three-tier quantitative framework
during the negotiation of the Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) or Quota Purchase
Agreement (QPA):

| Threshold Mechanism       | Brazilian Legal Context & Purpose                                                                                          | Standard Calibration (Transaction Value / EBITDA)                               |
| :------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Cláusula _De Minimis_** | Individual claim threshold. Claims below this value are disregarded and cannot be aggregated for indemnification.          | **0.05% to 0.1%** of EBITDA or Purchase Price (e.g., BRL 10k to BRL 100k).      |
| **Basket (Franquia)**     | The aggregate pool of all _De Minimis_-exceeding claims. Determines when the seller's indemnity obligations are triggered. | **1% to 2%** of the Purchase Price.                                             |
| **Cap Limit (Teto)**      | The maximum liability cap for the seller's general representations and warranties (R&Ws).                                  | **10% to 30%** of the Purchase Price (Special/Fundamental Caps are often 100%). |

### 1.1. Tipping vs. Deductible Baskets

- **Tipping Basket (First-Dollar Basket):** Once the cumulative value of
  indemnifiable claims exceeds the Basket threshold, the Seller is liable for
  the **entire** amount from the first BRL cent (retroactive liability).
- **Deductible Basket (Franquia Simples):** Once the Basket threshold is
  exceeded, the Seller is only liable for the amount that **exceeds** the
  threshold.
- _Audit Direction:_ For target companies with historical labor and civil
  litigation (highly common in Brazil), buyers should negotiate a **Tipping
  Basket** to ensure full recovery once the threshold is crossed.

## 2. Sector-Specific Threshold Guidelines

### 2.1. Civil Claims (_Contigências Cíveis_)

- **High-Volume Consumer Litigation:** For B2C targets (retail, telecom,
  fintech), individual consumer claims are usually small (BRL 2k - BRL 15k) and
  fall below the _De Minimis_ threshold. However, they represent systemic risks.
- **Contractual Disputes:** Materiality should be set at **0.5% of the
  transaction value** for individual cases. Focus must be on active or
  threatened litigation involving key suppliers, distributors, or intellectual
  property disputes.

### 2.2. Labor Claims (_Contingências Trabalhistas_)

- **Individual Thresholds:** Labor claims in Brazil are notoriously frequent.
  Individual claims should be cataloged if they exceed **BRL 50,000** in
  estimated loss.
- **Systemic Aggregation:** Multiple individual claims arising from the same
  operational failure (e.g., misclassification of outsourced workers, unpaid
  overtime due to a uniform policy shift) must be aggregated and treated as a
  single claim for _De Minimis_ purposes.

### 2.3. Tax Claims (_Contingências Tributárias_)

- **Zero De Minimis for Audits:** Due to the complexity of the Brazilian tax
  system (ICMS, ISS, PIS/COFINS, IRPJ/CSLL), any active tax assessment (_Auto de
  Infração_) must be analyzed regardless of the _De Minimis_ limit, as tax
  penalties in Brazil regularly reach **75% to 150%** of the principal amount.
- **Administrative vs. Judicial Litigation:** Assessments in the administrative
  phase (e.g., CARF or municipal councils) must be monitored closely. If the
  probability of loss is assessed as _probable_ or _possible_, they must be
  factored into the purchase price negotiation or escrow holdback.

---

## 3. Treatment of Minor but Recurrent Claims (Systemic Risks)

In Brazil, targets often have hundreds of minor, identical claims (particularly
labor and consumer disputes) that individually do not meet the _De Minimis_
threshold. Sellers will argue these should be discarded.

- **Systemic Risk Rule:** If a series of claims arise from a common origin
  (e.g., a standard company policy, the same type of service contract, or a
  single product defect), they must be **aggregated** as a single claim.
- **Integration with Valuation:** If the aggregated value of these minor claims
  exceeds the _De Minimis_ threshold, the entire group is admitted into the
  Basket. If the risk is ongoing, a structural adjustment to the EBITDA (and
  hence the valuation multiplier) should be demanded rather than relying solely
  on post-closing indemnification.

---

## 4. Escrow and Notarial Escrow (_Provimento CNJ nº 197/2025_)

Post-closing indemnification is typically secured by retaining **5% to 15%** of
the purchase price in an escrow account for a period matching the statute of
limitations (typically 5 to 6 years for tax/labor claims).

- **Traditional Bank Escrow:** Often slow to set up, costly, and subject to
  complex bank approvals.
- **Conta Notarial Vinculada (Notarial Escrow):** Under **Provimento CNJ nº
  197/2025** (originating from the Marco Legal das Garantias - Law 14.711/2023),
  Brazilian notary offices (_Tabelionatos de Notas_) can act as impartial
  depositaries.
  - **Impenhorabilidade (Asset Protection):** Funds deposited in a _Conta
    Notarial Vinculada_ are segregated from the notary’s and the parties'
    assets, rendering them immune to unrelated judicial seizures (_penhoras_).
  - **Objective Release Conditions:** The notary releases the funds based on
    objective, pre-agreed conditions (e.g., final judicial decision, proof of
    tax clearance certificate - CND). If a dispute arises, the notary suspends
    the release and records the event via an _Ata Notarial_, preventing bank
    bureaucracy.
  - _Recommendation:_ Strongly advocate for the use of _Conta Notarial
    Vinculada_ for transactions under BRL 100M to reduce transactional costs and
    execution time.
