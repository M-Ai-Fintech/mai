# Key Provisions of the Brazilian Joint-Stock Company Law (Lei das S.A. - Law 6.404/76)

This document serves as a detailed legal reference for acquisitions, mergers,
and corporate governance actions involving Brazilian Joint-Stock Companies
(_Sociedades Anônimas_ - S.A.) or Sociedades Limitadas (LTDAs) that adopt the
S.A. Law supletively (Art. 1,053, parágrafo único, Civil Code).

---

## 1. Mergers, Amalgamations, and Spin-offs

### Art. 224 & 225 - Protocol and Justification (_Protocolo e Justificação_)

Any merger (_incorporação_), consolidation (_fusão_), or spin-off (_cisão_) must
be initiated through a formal **Protocol** (agreement between the involved
companies) and a **Justification** (explanation of the economic and legal
reasons for the transaction).

- _DD Checklist:_ Verify that the protocol contains the exchange ratio of
  shares, the criteria for valuation of the assets/liabilities, and the draft of
  the new bylaws.

### Art. 227 - Merger (_Incorporação_)

The transaction where one or more companies are absorbed by another, which
succeeds them in all rights and obligations.

- **Valuation Report:** Requires the appointment of three independent experts or
  a specialized appraisal firm to value the net assets of the absorbed company
  (_laudo de avaliação a valor de patrimônio líquido ou a valor de mercado_).
- **Shareholder Approval:** Must be approved by a general meeting of both the
  acquiring and the target company.

---

## 2. Merger of Shares (_Incorporação de Ações_)

### Art. 252 - Structure and Requirements

- **Definition:** Unlike a standard merger where the target company ceases to
  exist, in an _incorporação de ações_, the target's shares are entirely merged
  into the acquirer's equity. The target becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary
  (_subsidiária integral_) of the acquirer, and the target's shareholders
  receive newly issued shares of the acquirer.
- **Protection of Minorities:** Dissenting shareholders of both the acquirer and
  the target may have withdrawal rights under certain conditions.
- **Valuation Comparison:** The exchange ratio must be justified and compared
  based on at least two criteria (e.g., net book value, market value, or
  discounted cash flow) to ensure the minority shareholders of the target are
  not unfairly diluted.

## 3. Withdrawal Rights (_Direito de Retirada / Recesso_)

### Art. 137 - Grounds and Exercise

The right of a dissenting shareholder to withdraw from the company and receive a
refund for their shares if the general assembly approves major structural
changes.

- **Trigger Events:**
  1. Merger (_incorporação_) or consolidation (_fusão_) of the company.
  2. Merger of shares (_incorporação de ações_).
  3. Participation in a group of companies (Art. 265).
  4. Change in the company's primary corporate purpose (_objeto social_).
  5. Reduction of the mandatory dividend.
  6. Spin-off (_cisão_) that results in a change of corporate purpose, reduction
     of mandatory dividend, or participation in a group of companies.
- **Timeline:** The dissenting shareholder must exercise the right within **30
  days** of the publication of the minutes of the general meeting that approved
  the transaction.
- **Reconsideration Clause (Art. 137, § 3º):** If the payment of the withdrawal
  refunds threatens the financial stability of the company, the board of
  directors/officers can call a new general assembly within **10 days** after
  the 30-day exercise period ends to **reconsider or undo** the transaction.

### Art. 45 - Calculation of Reimbursement Value

- **Book Value Default:** Unless the bylaws specify otherwise, the refund is
  calculated based on the book value (_valor patrimonial_) of the shares,
  derived from the last approved balance sheet.
- **Bylaw Customization:** Bylaws may establish that the refund is based on the
  economic value (_valor econômico_ / Discounted Cash Flow) or market value.
- _M&A Risk:_ If the target's bylaws dictate economic value for withdrawal, a
  wave of dissenting shareholders can drain the target's cash post-signing,
  impacting the deal's economics.

## 4. Duties and Liabilities of Administrators (Arts. 153 to 158)

### Art. 153 - Duty of Care (_Dever de Diligência_)

The administrator must employ, in the exercise of their duties, the care and
diligence that an active and honest person usually employs in the administration
of their own affairs.

- _Application:_ This incorporates the **Business Judgment Rule** into Brazilian
  law. Directors are protected from liability for business failures if they
  acted in good faith, with adequate information, and without conflict of
  interest.

### Art. 154 - Duty of Loyalty (_Dever de Lealdade_)

The administrator must exercise their powers to achieve the company's objectives
and in its best interest, avoiding conflict of interest. They are prohibited
from:

- Using corporate opportunities for their own benefit.
- Receiving personal benefits from third parties due to their position without
  general assembly approval.

### Art. 155 - Duty of Secrecy & Insider Trading

Administrators must maintain confidentiality regarding any business opportunity
or non-public material fact. They are strictly prohibited from using privileged
information to trade securities (_insider trading_).

### Art. 156 - Conflict of Interest & Self-Dealing

An administrator cannot participate in any decision or transaction in which they
have a conflict of interest with the company.

- **Related Party Transactions:** If an administrator has a personal interest in
  a transaction, they must disclose it to the board, abstain from voting, and
  document the conflict in the minutes. The transaction must be conducted on
  arm's length terms.

### Art. 158 - Civil Liability of Administrators

- **General Rule:** Administrators are **not** personally liable for obligations
  they contract in the name of the company and within their regular management
  authority.
- **Personal Liability Exception:** An administrator is personally and jointly
  liable for damages caused if they act:
  1. Within their authority, but with negligence or willful misconduct (_culpa
     ou dolo_).
  2. In violation of the law or the company's bylaws (strict liability for
     damages).
- _M&A Action:_ During due diligence, obtain a copy of the D&O (Directors and
  Officers) insurance policy and check for historical claims or active
  administrative investigations by the CVM (for public S.A.s).
