# Key Provisions of the Brazilian Joint-Stock Company Law (Lei das S.A. - Law 6.404/76)

This document serves as a detailed legal reference for acquisitions, mergers, and corporate governance actions involving Brazilian Joint-Stock Companies (*Sociedades Anônimas* - S.A.) or Sociedades Limitadas (LTDAs) that adopt the S.A. Law supletively (Art. 1,053, parágrafo único, Civil Code).

---

## 1. Mergers, Amalgamations, and Spin-offs

### Art. 224 & 225 - Protocol and Justification (*Protocolo e Justificação*)
Any merger (*incorporação*), consolidation (*fusão*), or spin-off (*cisão*) must be initiated through a formal **Protocol** (agreement between the involved companies) and a **Justification** (explanation of the economic and legal reasons for the transaction).
*   *DD Checklist:* Verify that the protocol contains the exchange ratio of shares, the criteria for valuation of the assets/liabilities, and the draft of the new bylaws.

### Art. 227 - Merger (*Incorporação*)
The transaction where one or more companies are absorbed by another, which succeeds them in all rights and obligations.
*   **Valuation Report:** Requires the appointment of three independent experts or a specialized appraisal firm to value the net assets of the absorbed company (*laudo de avaliação a valor de patrimônio líquido ou a valor de mercado*).
*   **Shareholder Approval:** Must be approved by a general meeting of both the acquiring and the target company.

---

## 2. Merger of Shares (*Incorporação de Ações*)

### Art. 252 - Structure and Requirements
*   **Definition:** Unlike a standard merger where the target company ceases to exist, in an *incorporação de ações*, the target's shares are entirely merged into the acquirer's equity. The target becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary (*subsidiária integral*) of the acquirer, and the target's shareholders receive newly issued shares of the acquirer.
*   **Protection of Minorities:** Dissenting shareholders of both the acquirer and the target may have withdrawal rights under certain conditions.
*   **Valuation Comparison:** The exchange ratio must be justified and compared based on at least two criteria (e.g., net book value, market value, or discounted cash flow) to ensure the minority shareholders of the target are not unfairly diluted.

## 3. Withdrawal Rights (*Direito de Retirada / Recesso*)

### Art. 137 - Grounds and Exercise
The right of a dissenting shareholder to withdraw from the company and receive a refund for their shares if the general assembly approves major structural changes.

*   **Trigger Events:**
    1.  Merger (*incorporação*) or consolidation (*fusão*) of the company.
    2.  Merger of shares (*incorporação de ações*).
    3.  Participation in a group of companies (Art. 265).
    4.  Change in the company's primary corporate purpose (*objeto social*).
    5.  Reduction of the mandatory dividend.
    6.  Spin-off (*cisão*) that results in a change of corporate purpose, reduction of mandatory dividend, or participation in a group of companies.
*   **Timeline:** The dissenting shareholder must exercise the right within **30 days** of the publication of the minutes of the general meeting that approved the transaction.
*   **Reconsideration Clause (Art. 137, § 3º):** If the payment of the withdrawal refunds threatens the financial stability of the company, the board of directors/officers can call a new general assembly within **10 days** after the 30-day exercise period ends to **reconsider or undo** the transaction.

### Art. 45 - Calculation of Reimbursement Value
*   **Book Value Default:** Unless the bylaws specify otherwise, the refund is calculated based on the book value (*valor patrimonial*) of the shares, derived from the last approved balance sheet.
*   **Bylaw Customization:** Bylaws may establish that the refund is based on the economic value (*valor econômico* / Discounted Cash Flow) or market value.
*   *M&A Risk:* If the target's bylaws dictate economic value for withdrawal, a wave of dissenting shareholders can drain the target's cash post-signing, impacting the deal's economics.

## 4. Duties and Liabilities of Administrators (Arts. 153 to 158)

### Art. 153 - Duty of Care (*Dever de Diligência*)
The administrator must employ, in the exercise of their duties, the care and diligence that an active and honest person usually employs in the administration of their own affairs.
*   *Application:* This incorporates the **Business Judgment Rule** into Brazilian law. Directors are protected from liability for business failures if they acted in good faith, with adequate information, and without conflict of interest.

### Art. 154 - Duty of Loyalty (*Dever de Lealdade*)
The administrator must exercise their powers to achieve the company's objectives and in its best interest, avoiding conflict of interest. They are prohibited from:
*   Using corporate opportunities for their own benefit.
*   Receiving personal benefits from third parties due to their position without general assembly approval.

### Art. 155 - Duty of Secrecy & Insider Trading
Administrators must maintain confidentiality regarding any business opportunity or non-public material fact. They are strictly prohibited from using privileged information to trade securities (*insider trading*).

### Art. 156 - Conflict of Interest & Self-Dealing
An administrator cannot participate in any decision or transaction in which they have a conflict of interest with the company.
*   **Related Party Transactions:** If an administrator has a personal interest in a transaction, they must disclose it to the board, abstain from voting, and document the conflict in the minutes. The transaction must be conducted on arm's length terms.

### Art. 158 - Civil Liability of Administrators
*   **General Rule:** Administrators are **not** personally liable for obligations they contract in the name of the company and within their regular management authority.
*   **Personal Liability Exception:** An administrator is personally and jointly liable for damages caused if they act:
    1.  Within their authority, but with negligence or willful misconduct (*culpa ou dolo*).
    2.  In violation of the law or the company's bylaws (strict liability for damages).
*   *M&A Action:* During due diligence, obtain a copy of the D&O (Directors and Officers) insurance policy and check for historical claims or active administrative investigations by the CVM (for public S.A.s).
