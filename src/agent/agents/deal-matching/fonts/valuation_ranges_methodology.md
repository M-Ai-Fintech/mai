# Valuation Ranges Methodology in M&A Transactions

This document establishes the methodology for using valuation ranges (*faixas de valuation*) instead of single-point numbers to reduce pricing friction and facilitate deal matching.

---

## 1. Triangulation and the "Football Field" Chart

Relying on a single valuation figure creates an illusion of precision. A robust valuation uses a range built from three core methodologies:

1.  **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF):**
    *   *Lower Bound:* High WACC (incorporating size/liquidity risk premiums) and conservative terminal growth rate ($g$).
    *   *Upper Bound:* Target WACC and optimistic growth rate.
2.  **Comparable Public Multiples (Trading Comps):**
    *   *Lower Bound:* 25th percentile of peer group EV/EBITDA, adjusted for liquidity.
    *   *Upper Bound:* 75th percentile of peer group multiples.
3.  **Precedent Transactions (Deal Comps):**
    *   *Lower Bound:* Minimum historical multiple for similar assets.
    *   *Upper Bound:* Maximum historical multiple during peak cycles.

### The "Football Field" Synthesis
The overlapping ranges of these methodologies are plotted together:

```
Methodology              [----Valuation Range (BRL Millions)----]
------------------------------------------------------------------
DCF (WACC 14% - 17%)          [========= 85M - 110M =========]
Trading Comps (5.5x - 7.5x)       [====== 80M - 100M ======]
Precedent Trans. (6.0x - 8.0x)        [======= 90M - 115M =======]
------------------------------------------------------------------
Consensus Range (ZOPA)               [====== 90M - 100M ======]
```

The area of overlap defines the **Zone of Possible Agreement (ZOPA)**.

---

## 2. Strategic Benefits of Valuation Ranges

### A. Reducing Emotional Anchoring and Friction
*   **Single-Point Valuation:** If a buyer offers exactly BRL 90M, a seller who expects BRL 100M may feel insulted, stalling the negotiation.
*   **Valuation Range:** Presenting a range of BRL 85M to BRL 105M shifts the discussion to the underlying business drivers (e.g., growth rates and margins) rather than a simple debate over the price.

### B. Managing Expectation Mismatches
*   A valuation range allows both parties to discuss risk productively:
    *   *Low Range Scenario:* Reflects risks like client concentration or regulatory delays.
    *   *High Range Scenario:* Reflects growth opportunities and synergy realization.
*   By agreeing on the parameters of the range, negotiators can design structure-based solutions for different outcomes.

### C. Facilitating Deal Matching (The DealMatchingAgent SOP)
The matchmaker agent uses valuation ranges to maximize transaction volume:
1.  **Overlap Assessment:** Compare the buyer's maximum investment range with the seller's acceptable valuation range.
2.  **Structuring the Gap:** If the ranges do not overlap, but the gap is under 20%, the agent flags the deal as viable and recommends structured options:
    *   **Baseline cash payment** at the buyer's lower bound.
    *   **Contingent earn-out** that reaches the seller's upper bound if targets are met.

> [!NOTE]
> Moving from a fixed price to a valuation range is a key tool for managing negotiations. It shifts the discussion from price to risk, enabling structured agreements that align both parties' incentives.
